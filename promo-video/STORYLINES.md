# Promo video: three storylines

Viewer: someone who has never heard of deriva. Length: 30 s. Source: docs/index.html and PROMO.md only.
Nothing personal: no names, no "I"; hand labels are "checked by hand".

The one thing to remember: *a variable can keep its name while its question changes, and deriva shows you where.*
The one real piece of data that travels through every version: `p76`, whose wording changed in December 2020
from "BONO DE DESARROLLO HUMANO" to "BONO o PENSIÓN".

---

## A. Same name, different question (recommended)

**Device:** the feature as the hook, on one real question. **Form:** one frame that rebuilds (the questionnaire sheet never leaves the screen; it keeps rearranging).

The viewer watches `p76` being read round by round, sees the one word that changed light up red, then sees the same sheet turn into deriva's history row for all nine rounds. It feels like a careful reader catching something you would have missed.

- **First 2 s:** a big `p76` in a form-blue question box, with the 2019 wording under it: "¿Cuánto recibió … por el BONO DE DESARROLLO HUMANO?"
- **Beats:** hook (p76, 2019) → tension (round stamps tick 2019 → 2020, "Same name. Same question?") → payoff (Dec 2020: "BONO DE DESARROLLO HUMANO" is struck, "BONO o PENSIÓN" writes in, red dot fills) → reveal ("deriva" + "compares the dictionaries of two ENEMDU rounds") → proof (nine rounds as a row of marks, the 2021 redesign as a hatched break; the four answers: added, removed, cosmetic, real) → trust ("22 changes checked by hand. 0 real changes hidden.") → claim ("deriva shows what moved. You decide.") → end card (wordmark, `esst-prog2.github.io/deriva`).
- **On-screen lines:** "p76, June 2019" · "Same name in every round" · "Same question?" · "Dec 2020: not any more" · "deriva compares ENEMDU dictionaries" · "Nine rounds. Every change marked." · "Checked by hand: 0 real changes hidden" · "deriva shows what moved. You decide."
- **Look:** the promo page itself: white sheet on pale blue desk, form-blue rules and numbered boxes, Archivo + JetBrains Mono, red used only for the one real change.
- **Sound:** soft, steady music bed (free/local) plus a pencil tick on each mark, one firmer tick on the red dot. Muted by default on the page, play controls to turn it on.

## B. Nine dictionaries

**Device:** life before and after. **Form:** split screen to one.

Nine spreadsheet-like dictionary sheets crowd the frame, each laid out differently (different columns, file names, the 2021 redesign), and a highlighter hunts line by line for `p76`. Then they collapse into one row: deriva's history of `p76`, the red dot on Dec 2020. It feels like relief from tedious work.

- **First 2 s:** nine small sheets in a 3×3 grid, file names visible, a highlighter sweeping the first one.
- **Beats:** hook (nine sheets) → tension (layouts differ, the hunt stalls, "Read every question, one by one?") → reveal ("deriva") → proof (the grid folds into one history row; command `deriva 2021.xlsx 2025.xlsx` → "151 vs 139 variables, 12 removed, 22 changed") → payoff (red dot on Dec 2020, the BONO wording) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** starts in grey spreadsheet grids (the old way), ends on the white questionnaire sheet with form blue (the new way).
- **Sound:** busier ticking during the hunt, quiet when the grid collapses.
- Risk: the nine sheets are rebuilt to look like dictionaries, not real screenshots, so they would be labelled as illustrations.

## C. Big type, one red dot (the bold one)

**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** cut on the beat.

Huge white words on full-bleed form-blue fields cut on the music: "p76." "2019." "2025." "Same question?" Hard cut to white: "No." and a single red dot slams in. The rest of the film is the dot: it travels onto the history row, then becomes the full stop in "You decide."

- **First 2 s:** "p76" filling the frame, white on form blue.
- **Beats:** hook (p76) → tension (years cut fast) → twist ("No.", red dot) → reveal ("deriva") → proof (two words per cut: "added", "removed", "cosmetic", "real"; "22 checked by hand", "0 hidden") → claim → end card.
- **Look:** flat form-blue and white fields, Archivo at its widest, red dot as the only accent.
- **Sound:** needs a clear beat; the film is timed to it.
